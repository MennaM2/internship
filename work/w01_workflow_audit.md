## Part 2: Three Target Tasks & Success Definitions

### Task 1: Writing Clear ML Notebook Documentation
* **Target:** Collaborate with AI
* **Done Well Means:** Every markdown section clearly answers the 4 core framing questions (Decision, Action, Cost, Data) using precise, decision-support language without over-promising model capability.

### Task 2: Fast Error Debugging & Troubleshooting
* **Target:** Collaborate with AI
* **Done Well Means:** Identifying the root cause of Python/Pandas exceptions in under 3 prompts and understanding the fix rather than blindly copying code.

### Task 3: Technical Paper & Documentation Summarization
* **Target:** Delegate to AI with review
* **Done Well Means:** Extracting 3–5 actionable takeaways from technical documentation in under 5 minutes with zero hallucinated parameters or syntax.
